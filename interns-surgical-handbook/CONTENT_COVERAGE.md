## Deliberately not converted into universal rules

- Fixed Stony Brook rounding/clinic/OR times and discharge deadlines.
- Local paging numbers, EHR names, note-routing policies, dress customs, and service-specific scope of practice.
- The idea that a consult, procedure, medication, or transfusion may be handled without complying with service triage, local privileges, supervision, and safety policy.
- Unprofessional or disparaging language, humor aimed at colleagues, or tactics that punish staff for calling.
- Older work-hour descriptions where the current ACGME documents should be used instead.

## Editorial and safety status

- All cases are fictional, progress is stored only in the visitor's browser, and the site contains no patient chart feature.
- Content has not been independently peer reviewed. Medical learners should follow their institution's current supervision, escalation, handoff and documentation policies.
- A formal editorial review and guideline update process would be required before claims of educational effectiveness or any clinical deployment.
